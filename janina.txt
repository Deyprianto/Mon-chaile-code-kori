This Java code is a perfect and simple example of the Command Design Pattern.
Let me explain it in very simple words, like you're 10 years old
Imagine this real-life situation:
You have a TV remote and a TV.

You press "Power ON" button → TV turns on
You press "Power OFF" button → TV turns off

The remote doesn't know HOW the TV turns on/off.
It just knows: "When I press this button, do this action".
